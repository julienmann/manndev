How It Works

From first call
to launch day.

01
Brief & Discovery
A 30 to 60 minute call to understand your goals, your audience, and what success looks like. We take notes, ask the right questions, and come back with a clear direction. No obligation. If it's not the right fit, we'll say so honestly.
30 to 60 min call
02
Design
Before a single line of code is written, the site is designed. We establish a visual language (typography, palette, layout) that fits your brand. You see mockups first. We refine until it's right. Design and development never fight each other when they come from the same team.
Mockups · Visual identity
03
Build
Clean HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, or a modern stack like Next.js if the project calls for it. Every component is written from scratch, nothing templated. You get a live preview link from day one and can watch the site take shape in real time.
Next.js · TypeScript · Tailwind
04
Review & Refine
Two structured rounds of feedback built into every project. We test across browsers and devices, optimise for performance, and work through your notes. Lighthouse 100 is the target, not a vanity metric, but a proxy for a site that loads fast and is accessible to everyone.
Lighthouse · Cross-browser · A11y
05
Launch
Deployment to your domain, analytics wired up, and a walkthrough of how to manage it. You get full ownership of everything: code, hosting, accounts. No lock-in, no monthly platform fees unless you want them. We stay available for 30 days post-launch at no extra charge.
Deployed

Three standards every build ships to.

Inclusivity and Accessibility

A site that only works for some people isn't finished. Every build ships keyboard-navigable, screen-reader compatible, and colour-contrast checked to WCAG 2.2 AA, with reduced motion respected throughout. Accessibility isn't a final pass; it's built in from the first line of code, because the work should reach every visitor, not only the ones on a fast phone in good light.

WCAG 2.2 AA

Ethical Design

No fake urgency, no pre-ticked consent boxes, no surveillance pixels reporting your visitors to networks you never agreed to. Design here prioritises user well-being over conversion tricks: clear choices, honest interfaces, and visitors who feel respected.

No dark patterns

Sustainability and Performance

Lean, hand-coded sites use a fraction of the energy of bloated template builds, multiplied across every visitor, every day. Optimised performance and a lower carbon footprint aren't two different goals. They're the same thing.

Low carbon by default
